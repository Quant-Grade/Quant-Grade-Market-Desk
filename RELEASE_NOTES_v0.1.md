# Release Notes: Quant-Grade Market Desk v0.1

We are thrilled to officially tag the **v0.1 Frozen Release** of the Quant-Grade Market Desk.

This release represents the culmination of stabilizing the strictly-bounded local intelligence pipeline architecture. The ecosystem has officially transitioned from disjointed local analytical scripts into a cohesive, uncorruptible, deterministic orchestrator.

## Highlights
- **Real-Data Parquet Resolver:** Successfully bridged raw, external ingestion structures (`source=okx_ws/symbol=.../date=...`) to internal analysts dynamically without requiring persistent database bindings.
- **Structural Precedence (Fail-Closed Analysts):** VWAP Topography, Session Boundaries, and Liquidity Compressions now generate pristine structured JSON matrices.
- **Multi-Role Combiner:** Mathematical constraints are strictly formatted and locked before any LLM ever sees the data.
- **Local LLM Narrative Engine:** Integrated OpenAI-compatible local endpoints (e.g., LM Studio) to securely map quantitative metrics into readable English without inventing hallucinations.
- **Alert Policy Gate:** Instituted rigorous frequency bounds, spam limitations, and explicit semantic phrase blocklists ensuring absolute intelligence safety.
- **Safe Operations Abstraction:** Deployed `operator_run_profiles` and the `controlled_run_supervisor` daemon allowing infinite autonomous looping without modifying low-level execution states or risking unintended network egress.

## Stability Guarantees
All v0.1 foundational modules are officially marked as **FROZEN**. They adhere to rigorous boundary tests and strict internal schemas to guarantee operational consistency moving forward.
